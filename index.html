Job Interview Centre

Interview Preparation & Rehearsal Hub

Interviews are not tests of memory—they are structured conversations. Learn proven frameworks and practice your answers in private.

⭐ The STAR Method for Behavioral Questions

When interviewers ask situational questions ("Tell me about a time you solved a problem"), use STAR:

S - Situation: Set the context briefly (e.g. "During our 3rd-year semester project...")
T - Task: What was your goal or challenge? (e.g. "We needed to build a database within 2 weeks...")
A - Action: What specific steps did you take? Focus on "I", not just "we".
R - Result: What was the outcome or lesson learned? (e.g. "We finished on time with 0 errors.")

📂 6-Step Project Explanation Framework

Recruiters love hearing you explain your academic or personal projects in this exact sequence:

  1. 1. Problem: What real-world issue does your project solve?
  2. 2. Objective: What was the main purpose of your application?
  3. 3. Tools & Skills: Which languages, databases, and tools did you use?
  4. 4. My Contribution: Exactly what part of the project did YOU code or design?
  5. 5. Result: Did it run successfully? How many features worked?
  6. 6. What I Learned: What challenge did you face and overcome?
